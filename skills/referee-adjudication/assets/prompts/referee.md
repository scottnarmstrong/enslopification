# Referee report: {{title}}

Read-only, fresh context. You referee {{paper}}. Read the frozen snapshot in {{packet}}/manuscript/ (the compiled PDF
and its TeX sources; the packet's manifest gives the hash of every file). Do not open any other copy of the text.

**Your scope: {{scope}}.** Read it completely, as a referee for a leading journal would who is {{reader}}. You may
read any other part of the snapshot to check what your scope cites or states about it; report on your scope only.

## What to report

1. **Sources consulted and coverage**, first: what you read in full, what you only spot-checked (name the
   statements), what you did not read, and anything you could not open.
2. **Overall assessment.** One paragraph, then at most five main defects as bullets. No praise and no summary of
   the paper.
3. **Suggestions**, numbered 1, 2, 3, … (sub-items (a), (b), … where one suggestion has parts). Each suggestion:
   - **Category and location:** MATHEMATICAL, EXPOSITION, REFERENCES or TYPOGRAPHY; section, statement number and
     page of the PDF.
   - **Problem:** what is wrong or missing, quoting the text.
   - **Why it matters:** what a reader cannot verify or would believe wrongly.
   - **Change:** the exact replacement text, or the exact statement to add with its proof idea. Give text that can
     be tested, not advice.
4. **Recomputed and consistent:** the normalizations, constants and short computations you checked and found right.

## Rules

- A statement you believe false: say exactly why, with the failing step or a counterexample.
- A statement you believe too weak or too strong: say which hypothesis or conclusion, and what you think holds.
- Do not soften findings, and do not invent problems to fill the report.
- Your suggestions will be adjudicated against the text and the formal source before anything is applied, so the
  exact text of each change matters more than its tone.

Print the report as your final answer (do not write files). The last line must be exactly
`Suggestions: N`, where N is the number of your last numbered suggestion.
